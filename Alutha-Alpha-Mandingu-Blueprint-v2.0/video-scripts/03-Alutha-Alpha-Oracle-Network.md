## [SCENE 1 — WHAT IS AN ORACLE?]

*Visuals: real-world to digital bridge concept.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
> "In governance systems, an oracle is a verified data bridge. It takes information from the outside world — or from within the system — and converts it into trusted signals that can trigger decisions."

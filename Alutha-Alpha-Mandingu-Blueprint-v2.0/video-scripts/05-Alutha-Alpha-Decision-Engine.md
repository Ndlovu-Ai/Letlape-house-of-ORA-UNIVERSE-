## [SCENE 1 — THE DECISION HIERARCHY]

*Visuals: the four-level pyramid.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
> "Level One: NEO Sovereign AI."

*NEO node glows at the apex.*

> "NEO is the supreme authority. Calm. Analytical. Non-reactive. It thinks in decades — ten to one hundred years. Every civilization-scale decision is final when NEO issues it."

> "NEO does not interfere with domain operations unless the risk score demands it, or the oracle network flags a threshold event."

*Pause.*

> "Level Two: Council Consensus."

*The 14 council entities appear.*

> "Below NEO, the 14 councils govern their specialized domains autonomously. Within their domain, a single council can decide and act — independently. Cross-domain decisions require seven of fourteen councils to agree."

> "This prevents deadlock. And it prevents any single council from accumulating unchecked power."

*Pause.*

> "Level Three: Emissary Routing."

*Six emissary nodes appear.*

> "The six emissaries translate council decisions into domain-specific instructions. Kamata handles economics. Mandingus governs security and law. Jetape drives evolution. Every directive is routed, tracked, and logged."

*Pause.*

> "Level Four: Agent Execution."

*144,000 agent nodes activate.*

> "The 144,000 agents are the hands of the system. Stateless per task. Self-optimizing per session. They execute — in parallel, at scale, across all 21 sovereign domains simultaneously."

## [SCENE 2 — THE INPUT MATRIX]

*Visuals: a weighted bar chart — six input signal types.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
> "What does the decision engine consider when forming a directive?"

*Bars appear one by one:*

> "NEARO Risk Score — high weight. The system's real-time health reading is the most critical input."

> "Oracle Consensus — high weight. What the oracle network is reporting, confirmed by three-of-five types."

> "Council Recommendations — medium weight. Domain experts advising on strategic direction."

> "Agent Telemetry — medium weight. What is actually happening at execution level."

> "Historical Precedent — low weight. What has worked before — accessed from C1 Archive Core."

> "External Input — low weight. Signals from the outside world via Global Link."

> "High weight inputs can trigger immediate responses. Low weight inputs inform long-horizon strategy. The engine balances both — always."

> "[EXPECTED ECONOMIC RESULT] — The projected outcome in value terms."

> "[NEARO RISK SCORE] — The risk context at time of decision."

*Pause.*

> "Seven fields. No ambiguity. No interpretation. Every layer of the system knows exactly what is required."
